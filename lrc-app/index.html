The Mission

Lighthouse Run Club is Cherokee County's community run club — built on the belief that running is one of the most powerful tools for personal growth, and that community makes everything better. We exist to bring the scattered running community of Canton and Woodstock together into one unified, energized movement.

Our Values

01

Lead With Light

Respect, kindness, encouragement, and inclusion come first. Every person should leave a Lighthouse run feeling a little better than when they arrived.

02

Bring the Energy

We choose optimism, effort, and gratitude for the opportunity to move together.

03

Become Through the Miles

We use running to challenge ourselves, build confidence, and discover what we are capable of becoming.

04

Find Your Light

Look toward the light in the distance, dig deeper, and keep moving forward.

05

Lift the People Around You

When one person gets stronger, the whole community gets stronger.

06

Create Together

We welcome photos, videos, storytelling, and collaboration. Help tell the story of Lighthouse Run Club.

07

Keep Moving Forward

Progress over perfection. Whether it's your first mile or your fastest — keep moving forward.
