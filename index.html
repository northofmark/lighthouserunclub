Who We Are

Our Values

01

Lead With Light

We treat people the way we want to be treated. Respect, kindness, encouragement, and inclusion come first. Every person should leave a Lighthouse run feeling a little better than when they arrived.

02

Bring the Energy

We show up with a positive, can-do attitude. Some runs will feel effortless. Others will test us. Either way, we choose optimism, effort, and gratitude for the opportunity to move together.

03

Become Through the Miles

Running is about more than pace or distance. We use it to challenge ourselves, build confidence, strengthen discipline, and discover what we are capable of becoming.

04

Find Your Light

There will always be moments when the finish feels far away. We learn to look toward the light in the distance, dig deeper, and keep moving forward. We believe every person in this community has more potential inside them than they may realize.

05

Lift the People Around You

We don't just chase our own goals. We encourage the person beside us, celebrate each other's progress, and help one another keep going. When one person gets stronger, the community gets stronger.

06

Create Together

We welcome photos, videos, storytelling, creativity, and collaboration. Members are encouraged to create content together, share their journeys, celebrate the community, and help tell the story of Lighthouse Run Club.

07

Keep Moving Forward

We don't expect perfection. We value consistency, courage, curiosity, and progress. Whether you're running your first mile or chasing a goal you once thought was impossible — the objective is the same: keep moving forward.
